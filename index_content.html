Who's BedTime?

Hi — I'm BedTime. As of July 2025, I'm a recent high school graduate working on small projects over the summer. A friend dared me to do something with my free time, so I built this site to share what I learn: photography, music, languages (Japanese & German), and random experiments. If you're curious, stick around — I’ll post updates and little projects here.

The idea started when a friend said I couldn't possibly spend the summer jobless without doing a little project. After failing to come up with anything, I figured a website would be a simple but challenging way to use my free time — so here I am.

I'm in the limbo of teaching myself languages like Japanese and German (just like I did with English).

There’s usually a reason behind what I do. Learning languages and building this site are two of the few things I started without expecting anything in return, and it feels strange — I don't really know what will come of them.

I hope you enjoy the site and the content I'll be uploading in the coming months.
